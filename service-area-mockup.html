Service Radius Features

🗺️

Interactive Maps

Visual radius selection with real-time map preview. See your service area coverage with animated radius circles.

📏

Adjustable Radius

5-50 mile radius options. Customers set search distance, vendors set service coverage. Flexible and customizable.

📍

Distance-Based Matching

Vendors sorted by distance from customer. Shows exact mileage in recommendations. Prioritizes nearby professionals.

🎯

Smart Filtering

Only shows vendors within customer's radius AND customer within vendor's service area. Two-way matching validation.

📊

Area Statistics

See vendor count, average ratings, and coverage stats for your area. Make informed decisions about service radius.

🔄

Real-Time Updates

Radius changes update immediately. Vendor availability reflects service area. Dynamic matching as vendors adjust coverage. Live market conditions.

📈

Dynamic Radius Boost

Vendors can temporarily expand service area during slow periods. Increases job opportunities and market reach. Adjust back anytime.

🎯

Market Intelligence

Shows projected job count increase with radius expansion. Vendors see potential homeowners in expanded area. Data-driven decisions.

⚡

Customer Benefits

More vendors = more availability. Customers benefit when vendors boost radius. Better pricing competition. Faster service times.

🏢

Business Location

Vendors set primary business location. Radius calculated from business address. Multiple service zones coming soon.

💡

Smart Recommendations

System suggests optimal radius based on area density. Alerts if radius too small (no vendors) or too large (quality dilution).

🔒

Privacy Protected

Exact address hidden until booking confirmed. Vendors see general area, not specific location. Address revealed after acceptance.
